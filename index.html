Content Stream Architecture

Four always-on streams with defined sub-categories. Each stream maps to specific platform tiers, content types, and stakeholder owners.

Athletics
Pac-12 era milestone moments, game hype, fan engagement, and student-athlete storytelling.
  • Pac-12 transition (July 1 Day One Giving Campaign)
  • Back the Bobcats event (August)
  • All sports first Pac-12 home & away games
  • Pre-hype / home game countdown content
  • Wins vs. Pac-12 schools — unique showcase by sport
  • Homecoming week
  • Family Weekend (fall & spring)
  • Basketball half-court challenge games
  • Town games / ISD attendance
  • Championships coverage
  • Student-athlete highlights & staff features
  • Bobcat Days ticket tie-ins (2x/semester)
Core Institutional
Annual baseline content — always planned, shared with key stakeholders including Athletics and Alumni Association.
  • Student success & retention (registration, advising, student stories)
  • Student resources (Career Services, SLAC, Campus Rec, Counseling/Health)
  • Student engagement (Try Series, events, student orgs)
  • Office Hours podcast (faculty spotlight)
  • Alumni stories & milestones
  • Institutional announcements & leadership news
Academics
Research, faculty excellence, program highlights, and the academic calendar rhythms.
  • Faculty research & discoveries
  • Program/college spotlights
  • Student research & creative work
  • Commencement & graduation milestones
  • Academic award & recognition moments
  • Dean's List / honor roll recognition
  • Study abroad & experiential learning
Brand Awareness / Reputation
Elevating TXST's national profile, community identity, and Bobcat pride — shareable, top-of-funnel content.
  • Rankings & recognition moments
  • Campus life & beauty/pride content
  • Community & San Marcos identity
  • Alumni impact & notable achievements
  • Trend-responsive content (& cultural moments)
  • Campaign amplification (#TXSTFamily etc.)
  • Evergreen "Why TXST" storytelling

Platform Tiers

Not every post hits every channel. These tiers prevent the calendar from becoming an 8-channel repost machine.

Tier 1 — High Frequency
Core news feed platforms. Majority of content lands here. All 4 streams are active.
Instagram Twitter / X Facebook
Tier 2 — Selective
Audience-appropriate or emerging platforms. Core Institutional & Brand Awareness lead here.
LinkedIn Threads Bluesky
Tier 3 — Purpose-Built
Longer-form or community-specific. Athletics & Academics drive YouTube; Reddit is community/AMA-focused.
YouTube / Shorts Reddit

Content Types

Every post on the rolling calendar is tagged with one of three types.

Types: ● Planned Confirmed date, pre-produced. Lead time: 2–3 wks min. ● Reactive Respond in real time (wins, announcements, breaking news). ● Evergreen No expiry date — fill gaps, repurpose year-round.

Platform Guide & Content Notes

Channel-specific guidance for the TXST flagship accounts. Use this to brief the content team or inform a Canva template system.

Instagram
Tier 1. Visual-first. All 4 streams. High-frequency.
  • Feed posts: evergreen brand, milestone moments, student stories
  • Stories: daily touchpoints, polls, quizzes, countdowns
  • Reels: hype videos, quick wins, campus life moments
  • Primary for Athletics game-day atmosphere content
Twitter / X
Tier 1. Real-time, conversational. Athletics and news lead.
  • Live game coverage and score updates
  • Breaking news and announcements
  • Reactive content (wins, rankings, news drops)
  • Engagement threads and community interaction
Facebook
Tier 1. Alumni and family audience primary.
  • Event promotion (Family Weekend, Homecoming, Commencement)
  • Video content performs well — share Reels/Shorts here
  • Alumni-facing institutional and brand content
  • Longer-form content and links perform better than other platforms
LinkedIn
Tier 2. Professional audience. Academics and Institutional lead.
  • Faculty research, rankings, institutional achievements
  • Student career success and employer partnerships
  • Office Hours podcast episode launches
  • Pac-12 membership as reputational milestone
Threads / Bluesky
Tier 2. Selective. Brand voice and community building.
  • Conversational brand voice — less formal than LinkedIn
  • Selective Athletics moments (big wins, milestones)
  • Core Institutional highlights with community angle
  • Early adopter audience — lean into authentic voice
YouTube / Shorts
Tier 3. Purpose-built. Long-form and Shorts (repurposed Reels).
  • Office Hours podcast video version
  • Athletics highlight reels and long-form features
  • Shorts: repurpose Instagram Reels and TikTok-style content
  • Student stories and documentary-style features
Reddit
Tier 3. Community and AMA-focused. Requires authentic approach.
  • r/txstate community engagement — avoid corporate tone
  • AMA opportunities: student athletes, faculty, leadership
  • Answer questions authentically during key moments (Pac-12 launch)
  • Monitor for student sentiment and feedback signals
